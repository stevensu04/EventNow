About

Made for Brisbane's small and mid-sized events

Enterprise event platforms are built for 10,000-person conferences. EventNow is for the 30-person workshop, the 150-seat pitch night and the university seminar.

Why EventNow

It started as a university project about one question: what does a local organiser actually need? The answer was a tool that knows how many people each room holds, runs parallel sessions and gets people through the door. Not much else.

Local by design

Venue room capacities from South Bank to St Lucia are built in, so an event can't be overbooked.

Parallel tracks

Attendees choose the sessions they want, and each room enforces its own limit.

Fast for everyone

Registering takes under a minute and needs no account. Check-in updates instantly.

Under the hood

Built with

A server-rendered Django app with HTMX for the interactive parts. No heavy frontend framework.

Django 6 HTMX Bootstrap 5 PostgreSQL · Neon Django REST Framework django-allauth OpenAI API Vercel

EventNow is a demonstration event-management app. This page explains what it collects and why.

What we collect

  • When you register for an event: your name, email address and the sessions you choose.
  • When you create an account: your username and email address, plus a securely hashed password.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture URL, taken from your Google profile. EventNow never receives your Google password and requests no other access to your Google account.
  • Automatically: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

How it is used

Your details are used only to run the events you sign up for or host: confirming registrations, issuing tickets, and letting event organisers manage their attendee lists. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing.

Who can see it

An event's organiser can see the name, email address, sessions and attendance status of people registered for that event, and can export that list as a CSV. Organisers cannot see data for events they do not run.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon in Sydney, Australia, and served through Vercel. Both providers process data on our behalf.

AI features

Event titles and descriptions that organisers submit to the AI drafting tools are sent to OpenAI to generate text. Attendee details are never sent to OpenAI.

Your choices

You can ask for your data to be corrected or deleted at any time by opening an issue on GitHub. Because this is a demonstration project, data may be reset without notice.

Cookies

EventNow sets only the cookies it needs to work: a session cookie when you log in, and a CSRF cookie that protects forms. There is no advertising or third-party tracking.
